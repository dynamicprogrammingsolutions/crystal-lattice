## Known facts and supporting details

- This is a bounded progressive update, not the final positioning or
  information architecture.
- Observed comprehension and first-use problems take priority over adding
  additional feature sections.
- Remote-access content must remain within delivered bridge and owning-package
  security/commercial facts.
- Research confidence and participant/experiment language do not belong in
  public copy.
- A changed intended communication target must be recorded in the content
  specification before implementation.

## Scope boundaries

- Do not select the final long-term proposition or complete page architecture.
- Do not publish participant data, research labels, bridge target behavior, or
  unverified managed-service claims.
- Do not broaden into product implementation required by a finding; report and
  route that dependency.
- Store draft previews and captures under
  `landing_page/artifacts/research/120-early-user-and-remote-access-informed-website-update/`.

## Execution plan

Status: Draft

Review accepted early-user and remote-access findings, then select a bounded
set of changes with direct comprehension or product-use value. Update the
content specification before implementation when the intended target changes.

Improve current pages, FAQs, paths, compatibility explanations, and remote
content without waiting for the later full proposition experiments. Validate
locally and on relevant devices, then obtain approval before production
deployment.
