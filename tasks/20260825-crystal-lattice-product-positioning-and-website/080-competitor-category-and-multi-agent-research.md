# Research Competitors, Categories, and Multi-Agent Use

Compare major first-party coding agents, open unified agents, mobile
controllers, cross-agent utilities, plugin ecosystems, and adjacent agent
platforms to determine category language, normal expectations, differentiation,
and multi-agent switching value.

## Requirements checklist

- [ ] Products and offers are identified through attributable current sources
      and classified by product category and audience.
- [ ] Comparisons cover configured-agent/model breadth, provider/account
      options, session ownership, mobile continuity, extensibility,
      transparency, organization, pricing, proof, and first-use path.
- [ ] Official products, compatible alternatives, utilities, workaround
      substitutes, frameworks, and managed services remain distinct.
- [ ] Claims about standard versus model-family-specific tools, prompts, or
      behavior are verified rather than assumed.
- [ ] Findings identify established expectations, defensible combinations,
      weakened differentiators, and experiment questions.

## Known facts and supporting details

- Official coding agents, compatibility-oriented alternatives, unified/open
  agents, mobile controllers, utilities, frameworks, and generic workarounds
  solve different product jobs.
- A direct page is authoritative for a product's current public claim; search
  snippets and third-party summaries are discovery aids.
- Product claims do not establish actual quality, adoption, revenue, or user
  satisfaction.
- Public absence from one page does not prove feature absence.
- Mobile continuity is now a common first-party expectation, so differentiation
  must be evaluated across the complete workflow and product combination.
- Claims about one standard tool/prompt surface versus model-family-specific
  behavior require direct verification.

## Scope boundaries

- Do not contact providers, submit forms, request quotes, create accounts, or
  make purchases.
- Do not rank products by subjective quality or infer market share.
- Do not treat generic networking workarounds as direct coding-agent products.
- Do not change public comparison content or the content specification.
- Store downloaded pages, screenshots, and caches under
  `landing_page/artifacts/research/080-competitor-category-and-multi-agent-research/`.
