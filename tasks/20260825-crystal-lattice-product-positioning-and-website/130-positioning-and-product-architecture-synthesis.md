# Synthesize Positioning and Product Architecture

Combine product truth, feature opportunities, search, community, competitor,
early-user, and remote-access evidence into a decision-oriented set of product
categories, primary propositions, audience priorities, product relationships,
and alternatives for testing.

## Requirements checklist

- [ ] Source findings are synthesized without merging incompatible counts,
      confidence, or evidence roles.
- [ ] Candidate product categories and propositions identify audience, job,
      value, proof, limitations, and counterevidence.
- [ ] The relationship among the coding agent, configured-agent profiles,
      desktop/mobile clients, plugin/customization path, open source, and
      managed remote access is explicit.
- [ ] One recommended experiment set is selected by decision value while
      preserving credible alternatives and weakened hypotheses.
- [ ] The synthesis does not write final website copy or approve untested
      pricing and claims.

## Known facts and supporting details

- Evidence packages use different units and cannot be summed into one market
  score.
- Search language, community pain, competitor packaging, product readiness,
  observed user behavior, and willingness-to-pay evidence may disagree.
- The synthesis must choose decision-critical alternatives rather than make
  every supported value a co-equal homepage proposition.
- The coding-agent-first baseline remains until evidence justifies changing
  it.
- Public copy, final page architecture, and untested pricing remain downstream
  work.

## Execution plan

Status: Draft

Reconcile authoritative findings and limitations into a common decision
matrix. Score or compare directions across comprehension, user pain,
differentiation, product readiness, proof, reachability, switching burden,
retention, monetization, and maintenance risk without reducing every source to
one numeric score.

Produce a recommended architecture, alternatives, disagreement log, claim
boundaries, and experiment brief. Update durable research synthesis, but leave
the content specification unchanged until a target decision is approved.

- Open questions: final primary audience, product naming, general-purpose
  prominence, transparency default, customization promise, and managed-service
