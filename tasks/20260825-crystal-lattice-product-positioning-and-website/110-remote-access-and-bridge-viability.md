# Research Remote Access and Bridge Viability

Evaluate the user job, direct alternatives, self-operated path, managed-service
bundle, trust requirements, willingness to pay, and website presentation for
Crystal Lattice remote mobile continuity.

## Requirements checklist

- [ ] Research distinguishes direct local-network connection, a user-operated
      Crystal Lattice bridge, and a future managed bridge.
- [ ] Direct coding-agent mobile/remote products are separated from generic
      networking workarounds.
- [ ] The analysis covers frequency of remote use, desired interaction,
      notifications, multiple computers/devices, trust, privacy, encryption,
      reliability, setup burden, and support expectations.
- [ ] Managed-service naming, bundle, trial, price unit, and willingness-to-pay
      hypotheses are tested without launching or charging for an unready
      service.
- [ ] Findings produce a proceed, change, defer, or stop recommendation and
      safe current website wording.

The runtime is described as Crystal Lattice running on the user's computer.
Only the remote bridge is user-operated/self-hosted. Generic VPN or tunnel
tools may be studied as willingness-to-pay workarounds, not presented as
equivalent coding-agent products.

## Known facts and supporting details

- Crystal Lattice runs on an ordinary computer; only the relay service is
  user-operated/self-hosted.
- Direct same-network connection and remote bridge connection are different
  product paths.
- First-party coding agents and dedicated mobile controllers are direct
  alternatives; generic networking tools are workaround substitutes relevant
  to willingness to pay.
- Current public hosted-service, identity, E2EE, entitlement, purchase, quota,
  and production-readiness work remains incomplete unless owning tasks provide
  accepted evidence.
- Interest, waitlist signup, stated price acceptance, and payment are
  materially different evidence.

## Execution plan

Status: Draft

Combine competitor, community, early-user where available, product, and
workaround evidence. Define remote-use jobs such as supervision, full chat,
approval, notification, file access, or multi-computer switching.

Test managed-service concepts at appropriate fidelity, such as interviews,
waitlist interest, pricing comprehension, or non-charging fake doors. Keep all
claims conditional on current bridge security, identity, E2EE, purchase, and
release work.

### Implementation decisions and open questions

- Decision: do not market the runtime as user infrastructure.
- Decision: the open local and user-operated bridge paths remain visible.
- Recommendation: test an outcome-oriented working name rather than assuming
  “hosted bridge” is the final product name.
- Open questions: required managed bundle, acceptable price, free tier/trial,
  multi-connection value, and minimum trust guarantees.
