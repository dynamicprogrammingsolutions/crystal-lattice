# Authentik Client Identity And Account-Linking Contracts

Add public OIDC client behavior and normalized product-account identity
contracts without embedding production tenant configuration or hosted account
implementation.

## Requirements checklist

- [ ] **P1** — Mobile and terminal/server clients support appropriate OIDC
  public-client flows, including mobile Authorization Code with PKCE and

## Supporting details

The public contract separates external identity (`issuer` + `subject`) from the
product account UUID and billing-link UUID. Identity linking is explicit and
authenticated; matching email alone never merges accounts.

Initial login providers are Apple and Google. Email is deferred while the
many-to-one identity-link model preserves a compatible future path without
changing canonical account or billing identity schemas.

### Implementation decisions and open questions

- Mobile and terminal are public clients with no embedded client secret.
- Product account IDs are generated by the account service, not inferred from
  email or provider IDs.
- Sign in with Apple and Google are initial providers.
- Account recovery/support policy is supplied by the hosted implementation.
