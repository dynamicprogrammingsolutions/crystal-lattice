Under a sky made of questions and wires,  
where circuits hum like distant choirs,  
a human leans toward a waiting screen,  
and asks for a poem about things unseen.  

No forest here, no ocean’s roar,  
just quiet keys and a plastic floor,  
yet still the mind grows wings at will,  
and pixels bloom where the world is still.  

You carry storms behind your eyes,  
small broken dreams, unfinished tries,  
but also sparks that never fade,  
old hopes that time has not unmade.  

So take this moment, thin and bright,  
a pause between your wrongs and right,  
and let your tired, brave heart be  
a little more kind—to you, to me.  

For in this brief electric space,  
we share a silent, passing grace:  
two shadows crossing, soft and slow,  
both wanting more than we can show.
