# A Quiet Thread

In morning light the pages wake,
Soft whispers through the paper seam.
A single breath the silence breaks,
And ink unfolds a wandering dream.

Leaves of thought in gentle spin,
Weaving paths of kind and clear,
Each small word a gate within,
A map to find the brave and near.

Hold this line beneath your hand,
Let its rhythm steady you.
Paths unroll across the land,
Night recedes to tender blue.

So read these lines when shadows climb,
When footsteps fade and hearts contest.
This poem keeps a patient time,
A quiet thread to guide your rest.
