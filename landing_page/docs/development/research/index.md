# Product Website Research

This directory preserves durable supporting knowledge for Crystal Lattice
website content and positioning.

Research pages are not current product documentation and do not define the
website target. They support later content decisions and specification
updates.

## Start Here

- [Positioning and product baseline](positioning-and-product-baseline.md):
  verified starting facts, current observations, hypotheses,
  recommendations, and open questions.
- [Research method](research-method.md): evidence types, provenance, review,
  confidence, privacy, pilot, and content-promotion rules.
- [Evidence index](evidence-index.md): authoritative local inputs and initial
  external source routes.
- [Visual communication and reproducible assets](visual-communication-and-reproducible-assets.md):
  visual proof hypotheses and the retained reproducible asset-pipeline model.
- [Initial planning discussion retention map](discussion-retention-map.md):
  where important planning points are stored, partially retained, or
  intentionally omitted.
- [Current product and compatibility baseline](findings/current-product-and-compatibility-baseline.md):
  accepted configured-profile, provider, feature, tool, client, limitation,
  and canonical website-draft findings.

Retained normalized records, manifests, datasets, and review ledgers belong
under `landing_page/research/`. Disposable raw responses, browser output,
caches, generated previews, and experiment output belong under the ignored
`landing_page/artifacts/` directory.

Future findings pages are added only when a research workstream produces
substantive durable conclusions.
