## Contact support

Email
[support@dynamicprogrammingsolutions.com](mailto:support@dynamicprogrammingsolutions.com)
for help with the Crystal Lattice mobile app, installation, or connection
problems. Support correspondence is retained as business communication as
described in the [privacy policy](/privacy).

Do not send API keys, bridge or cloud connection tokens, private conversation
content, or other secrets.

## Information to include

To help diagnose a problem, include:

- The Crystal Lattice app version and iOS or Android version.
- The connection method: manual address, local discovery, self-hosted bridge,
  or self-hosted cloud connection.
- The Crystal Lattice runtime version and operating system.
- The steps that caused the problem and the exact error message.
- Screenshots or logs only after removing tokens, credentials, private messages,
  and other sensitive information.

## Self-hosted connections

The mobile app requires a Crystal Lattice runtime that you or your organization
operates. Dynamic Programming Solutions Kft. does not operate a public
production bridge or cloud-agent service. Confirm that your phone can reach the
self-hosted runtime, bridge, or cloud environment before reporting a connection
problem.

See the [mobile app page](/mobile) for supported connection methods and the
[repository documentation](https://github.com/dynamicprogrammingsolutions/crystal-lattice#readme)
for installation and self-hosting instructions.

## Report a software issue

For reproducible software defects that do not contain private information, you
can open a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/dynamicprogrammingsolutions/crystal-lattice/issues).
Security-sensitive reports and reports containing personal data should be sent
by email instead of posted publicly.

about local storage, self-hosted services, third-party AI providers, and support
